How to use

  • Double-click the picker to invert colors (white ↔ black)
  • RGB is actually a button — drag along the XYZ axes to pick colors
  • RGB letters show/hide randomly on hover — handy for screenshots
  • Hover the picker to reveal corner buttons: ◣ Random color · ◢ Reset
